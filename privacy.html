Grimbit: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Grimbit ("the game") is made by Oskars Galicins ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles. Contact: galicins.dev@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (heroes, gear, stages, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers and have no accounts, so we never receive your save data. Settings → Reset progress deletes it; uninstalling the game deletes it too.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the game which items you own so it can unlock them and restore them after a reinstall. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game sends your best scores (Abyss floor, Power, enemies slain, waves cleared, bosses slain, Souls earned, fastest campaign time) to Google's leaderboards. Your Play Games name and avatar appear next to them, and other players can see them. Google handles this under its own privacy policy, and you can change your Play Games profile visibility in the Play Games app. If you don't sign in, nothing is sent.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads only play when you tap a button to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • information about your device and its approximate location (from your IP address). The game never asks for Android's location permission, so it cannot read your precise (GPS) location, even though Google's consent message lists precise geolocation as a possible use;
  • ad interactions (impressions and clicks), and diagnostic data.

It uses these to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. If you are in the UK, the EEA or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

News and update check

When the game starts it downloads a small public news file from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) to show announcements and tell you when a newer version is out. This request sends nothing about you or your progress; like any web request, GitHub receives your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement: docs.github.com

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Analytics

The game contains no analytics of its own.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
